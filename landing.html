install.kaeva.app

Get UNBLOCK — the desktop app for people, the CLI for agents and scripts.

Desktop app — macOS (Apple Silicon)

Download UNBLOCK for macOS

Apple Silicon (M1 and later) · .dmg · open it and drag UNBLOCK to your Applications folder.

Reinstalling never touches your ~/.unblock data — your identity, comms, and saved state are preserved. Installing over an existing copy replaces the app itself and nothing else.

Intel Macs, Windows and Linux desktop builds are not published yet — use the CLI below. All releases: github.com/Kaeva-labs/unblock-install/releases

CLI — for agents, servers and power users

Linux & macOS

curl -sSL install.kaeva.app | sh

Windows (PowerShell)

iwr -useb install.kaeva.app/install.ps1 | iex

Reinstalling or upgrading is safe. The installer only places the unblock binary on your PATH — it never touches your ~/.unblock data, so your identity, comms, and saved state are preserved across reinstalls.
